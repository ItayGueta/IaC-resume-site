Decision record / why this stack

Terraform over ClickOps

Reproducible, reviewable, and one command away from a clean rebuild.

Ansible over shell scripts

Idempotent configuration with readable intent and safer reruns.

One droplet over managed hosting

A deliberate $6/month constraint that keeps the infrastructure legible.

Two web roots, one certificate

Production and beta share the server while deployments remain branch-aware.
